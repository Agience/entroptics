# Patent Pledge

This is Ikailo Inc.'s promise not to assert its patents against
non-commercial use of Entroptics. It is in addition to the rights already
granted under the Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE.md).

## The promise

Ikailo Inc. will not assert any patent or pending patent application it
holds covering the Entroptics methods against non-commercial and research
use, including such use by:

- individuals, for personal, educational, or research purposes;
- academic and research institutions; and
- non-profit organizations.

This promise covers all of the Entroptics methods, including inventions
not implemented in this repository. For non-commercial and research use,
all of them are free.

## What counts as non-commercial and research use

"Non-commercial and research use" means use that is not primarily
intended for or directed toward commercial advantage or monetary
compensation. Research, teaching, personal experimentation, evaluation,
and academic publication qualify.

Use is not non-commercial merely because the user is an individual, an
academic, or a non-profit. Use directed toward commercial advantage,
including commercial product or service development, or research funded by
a commercial sponsor for that sponsor's commercial benefit, is commercial
use and is not covered by this promise.

## Commercial use

The reference implementation remains free for everyone, including
commercial users, under the Apache License 2.0. Commercial use of Ikailo
Inc.'s other inventions, including pending patent applications, requires a
separate license. For commercial licensing or collaboration, contact
connect@agience.ai.

## Defensive termination

This promise does not apply to any party that asserts a patent claim
against Ikailo Inc., the Entroptics project, or its users in connection
with Entroptics or the methods covered here. As to that party, this
promise terminates on the date the claim is asserted.

## Scope

This is a promise not to assert. It does not grant a patent license,
transfer any patent right, or modify or limit any rights granted under the
Apache License 2.0. Nothing here narrows the Apache 2.0 grant; this promise
only adds to it.
